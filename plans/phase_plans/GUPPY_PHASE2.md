# Phase 2 — Concept Datasets

## What was built

`rfm_guppy/concepts.py` — loads the `arman-bd/guppylm-60k-generic` HuggingFace dataset, enumerates its 60 categories, defines three binary concept contrasts, builds class-balanced train/val/test splits with no prompt leakage, and saves each as a JSON manifest to `rfm_guppy/cache/`.

---

## Key discovery: severe prompt duplication in `input` field

The guide says to use the `input` text as the prompt, but the dataset's user messages are generated from tiny fixed pools (e.g., the `food` category has just 18 user-message templates, of which one is parameterised with 17 food types — giving ~34 unique user inputs across 950 rows). Most emotion/state categories have only **4–5 unique user inputs** repeated ~200+ times each.

This makes it impossible to split on `input` alone: we cannot produce non-overlapping train/val/test sets of ≥100 examples per class from 4–34 unique inputs.

**Resolution:** use the **full ChatML exchange** (user turn + Guppy response) as the prompt:

```
<|im_start|>user
{input}<|im_end|>
<|im_start|>assistant
{output}<|im_end|>
```

Guppy's responses are generated by template composition (pick × pick × pick over vocabulary pools of 6–15 options each), yielding hundreds to thousands of unique responses per category. This brings unique exchange counts to 40–940+ per category (see "Unique exchanges" column above). When passed to `extract_activations` with `format_chatml=False`, the model processes the complete conversation and the last-token activation (at the closing `<|im_end|>`) encodes the full concept context.

The three concepts are sized to respect what is actually available:

## Three binary concepts defined

### C1 — `food` (n_per_class = 300)

| Class | Categories | Approx. unique exchanges |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Positive (label 1) | `food`, `taste` | ~959 |
| Negative (label 0) | `weather`, `seasons`, `tv`, `music`, `glass`, `rain`, `outside` | ~310 |

Guppy's food responses are highly excited and food-lexicon-rich ("yes. always yes. food is the best thing. i promise to eat all of it."). The negative categories cover abstract, inanimate topics where no food references appear.

**n_per_class = 300** (capped by negative class's 310 available unique exchanges).

### C2 — `valence` (n_per_class = 100)

| Class | Categories | Approx. unique exchanges |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Positive (label 1) | `happy`, `excited`, `love`, `curious` | ~160 |
| Negative (label 0) | `scared`, `fear`, `bored`, `tired` | ~160 |

Maps to the paper's "moods" concept class. All eight categories have only 40 unique exchanges each (4 user messages × 10 response templates), so we pool four categories per side to reach 160 unique each.

**n_per_class = 100** (well within available 160).

### C3 — `env_social` (n_per_class = 300)

| Class | Categories | Approx. unique exchanges |
|-------|-----------|--------------------------|
| Positive (label 1) | `water`, `filter`, `temp_hot`, `temp_cold`, `algae` | ~2,830 |
| Negative (label 0) | `greeting`, `bye`, `friends`, `visitors`, `lonely` | ~2,756 |

Contrasts tank/environmental language ("the water feels clear today", "the filter hums") against social/greeting language ("hello.", "you're my friend. you bring food."). Both pools are very large, giving clean splits.

**n_per_class = 300**.

## Splits and leakage check

All three datasets use a **60 / 20 / 20** split (train / val / test) computed on deduplicated unique exchanges per class:

| Concept    | N/class | Train | Val | Test |
|------------|---------|-------|-----|------|
| food       | 300     | 360   | 120 | 120  |
| valence    | 100     | 120   | 40  | 40   |
| env_social | 300     | 360   | 120 | 120  |

Within each split: labels are **exactly balanced** (pos == neg confirmed programmatically). Across splits: prompt set intersections are **empty** (confirmed with `assert not (train_prompts & val_prompts)` etc.).

## Notes for later phases

- **`format_chatml=False`**: all prompts in concept JSON files are already complete ChatML exchanges. Pass this flag to `extract_activations` to avoid double-wrapping.
- **`valence` is the smallest dataset** (100/class → 40 val, 40 test). RFM should still work at this scale given the strong template signal, but AUROC confidence intervals will be wide.
- **`env_social` has the largest available pool**: if more training examples are needed for RFM, `n_per_class` can be increased up to ~2,756 without affecting split integrity.
- **Keyword lexicons** (`CONCEPT_LEXICONS`) are defined in `concepts.py` for Phase 6 use — these are coarse but language-model-free evaluation proxies.
